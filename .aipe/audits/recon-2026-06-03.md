## 3. PATH — track, signal, gap, claim

**Track:** **classical-ML + on-device** is contrl's load-bearing AI/ML track today. LLM-app is the planned-future track; until Phase 4 ships, leading with it in interviews is a liability.

**Strongest signal (the competency that, pushed to L3, becomes a centerpiece):**

→ **On-device inference + the deterministic harness around it.** This is the only competency at L1 today, and it has a clean L1→L2→L3 ladder:
- **L1 (today):** MediaPipe runs; reps get counted; happy path works.
- **L2 (one drill away):** build an accuracy eval against a 10-video labeled set; characterize where MediaPipe + FSM disagrees with ground truth; identify a specific failure mode (e.g., partial occlusion → false rep, or fast tempo → missed rep).
- **L3 (defensible):** describe the failure, the fix tried (e.g., higher visibility threshold, slower smoothing alpha, FSM hysteresis tuning), the alternative rejected (a more elaborate model, more landmarks), and the eval evidence that settled the tradeoff. Hold it under push-back.

This is the **eval-first** move from the drill starter library, adapted to contrl's shape. It is the single highest-leverage move available.

**Load-bearing gap:** **evals.** Without any measurement surface, every competency is capped at L1. The eval drill below lifts the ceiling on every track simultaneously.

**Claim-vs-reality calibration for interviews:**

- **Honest claim today:** "I shipped an on-device pose detection pipeline with a hand-built FSM rep counter. I haven't yet built the eval harness around it — that's my next move." (L1 — survives.)
- **Aspirational claim with evidence at the drill's end:** "I built the accuracy eval, found that fast-tempo reps drop ~12% accuracy because the smoothing factor was over-tuned for stability, retuned to recover 9%, and documented the remaining gap as a buildable target." (L3 — defensible.)
- **Liability claim to AVOID:** "I designed an agentic coaching system" — the code isn't there. Even a great design doc reads as scaffolded under questioning. Wait until Phase 4 ships, then drill it.
